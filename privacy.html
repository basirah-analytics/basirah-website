Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026. What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Who we are

Basirah Analytics is operated as a sole proprietorship by an individual based in India. You can reach us at hello@basirahanalytics.com. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Basirah Analytics.

What we collect

Contact details you give us. Your name, email address, your phone number if you message us on WhatsApp, and anything you choose to include when you contact us or book a call.

Files you upload to Basira AI. Covered in full in our Basira AI data handling statement, and summarised below.

Technical data. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It is cookieless, it does not track you across other websites, and it does not identify individual visitors. We do not use tracking cookies or any other analytics, and this site stores nothing in your browser. Our hosting providers process standard server request data, including IP addresses, as part of delivering and protecting the service. Basira AI also holds your IP address in memory for a short period to enforce its usage limits. It is never written to disk and is discarded automatically.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to deliver the services you ask for, to produce the analysis you request, and to protect the service from abuse. We do not sell your data. We do not share it except with the service providers listed below, and only as far as they need it to do their job.

Basira AI data handling

Files you upload to Basira AI are processed in memory to produce your analysis and are not written to our servers at any point. To generate the written insights, a compact statistical summary of your file is sent to the Google Gemini API. That summary contains your column names, aggregate figures such as totals and averages, and the most frequent values in each text column. It does not contain your file or its rows. The full detail, including what we log, is in the Basira AI data handling statement.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website, and provides the visit counts described above. Render hosts the Basira AI application. Google processes the statistical summary described above through its Gemini API to generate written insights.

If you contact us by email or on WhatsApp, that message is handled by the provider of that service under its own terms.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and client data only as long as we need it to provide the service and to meet our legal obligations, then delete it. Files uploaded to Basira AI are never retained, because they are never stored in the first place.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EU, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we process it. To exercise any of these, email hello@basirahanalytics.com and we will respond. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

We are based in India. If you are in the UK or the EU, your data will be processed outside your region, both by us and by the service providers listed above. We take reasonable steps to protect it in line with applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
