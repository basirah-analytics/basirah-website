Before you book

We are in different timezones. Will that work?
Yes. We are on IST (UTC+5:30) and keep our evenings and late nights open for international calls, which lines up with the working day across the USA, UK, and Europe. Between calls we work asynchronously, so you are never waiting on the clock to get an answer.
How do international clients pay?
By Wise or PayPal. Clients in India can pay by UPI, GPay, or PhonePe. We invoice per project against the written quote, so there is nothing to work out afterwards.
How long does a project take?
It depends on how much data there is and what state it is in, which is what the free call is for. We give you a timeline in writing with your quote before any work starts, and we would rather quote honestly than promise a number we cannot hold.
Is our data safe with you?
We keep your data confidential and we do not use it as an example or a case study without your written permission. We keep it only as long as we need it to do the work, then delete it. Files you upload to Basira AI are handled separately: they are processed in memory and never stored, and the data handling page sets out exactly what happens to them, including the one thing that leaves our servers.
What if our data cannot answer the question?
We tell you on the first call. We would rather say so upfront than bill you for a report that hedges. That honesty is the whole point of the name.
