A bit more about how we work

We come at analysis from a finance background: our founder is completing an MBA in Finance, so we read the numbers behind a business rather than just the charts drawn on top of them. That means we start from the business question, not the tool. What makes money. What quietly costs more than it should. What is about to become a problem while everyone is looking the other way. Then we work back to the data to answer it.

We try to be honest about what data can and cannot tell you. If the numbers will not support the question, we would rather say so on the first call than bill you for a report that hedges. That honesty is the whole point of the name.
