Five ways I help you see your business clearly. Every project starts with the question you need answered, not the tool.

Most owners need two or three of these, not all five. We work out which ones fit your problem on the first call.

01Service

Sales & Revenue Analysis

You know sales are happening. You just can’t see which ones are worth it.

Every sale looks fine on its own. The full picture is harder to see: which products, days, and places actually make you money, and which quietly waste your effort. I put it all in one view so you can push what works and drop what does not.

  • The products and services that truly earn
  • Your best days, seasons and locations
  • What is quietly draining time and money
  • A clear list of what to do more of
2 to 4 weeks
02Service

Business & Financial Analysis

Money comes in and goes out. The hard part is seeing where.

You feel whether a month was good or bad. What you cannot always see is why. I show you what earns, what quietly costs you, and whether your prices are set right, so your next money decision is one you can stand behind.

  • Profit and margins, in plain numbers
  • Whether your pricing is helping or hurting
  • Costs that eat more than they should
  • A simple forecast for the months ahead
2 to 5 weeks
03Service

Customer Insights & Retention

Some customers come back. Most don’t. Do you know which is which?

It costs far more to win a new customer than to keep one you already have. I show you who your best customers are, who is slipping away, and where a little effort would keep them, so your marketing goes where it counts.

  • Who your most valuable customers really are
  • Who is drifting away, before they are gone
  • Repeat buyers versus one-time visitors
  • Where to focus to keep more of them
2 to 4 weeks
04Service

Dashboards & Live Reporting

You shouldn’t have to ask someone for your own numbers.

Most owners wait on a person or a spreadsheet to know how things are going. I build you one clear screen that shows the health of the business at a glance, kept up to date, that anyone on your team can read without me.

  • One clear view of the whole business
  • Always current, no manual updates
  • The same number means the same thing to everyone
  • Simple enough for the whole team to use
3 to 5 weeks
05Service

Automation

You rebuild the same report every week. That is time you never get back.

If a report takes the same steps every time, a person should not be doing it. I set your reporting up to build and update itself, so the numbers are ready before you ask and nobody loses a day to copy-paste.

  • The weekly manual report, gone
  • Numbers that refresh on their own
  • One reliable flow from data to report
  • Hours handed back to your team
2 to 4 weeks

      about

      Why Basirah?

      Most businesses are not short of data. They are short of a clear picture.

      The numbers are already there, scattered across a till system, a spreadsheet, an accounting package, someone’s inbox. What’s missing is someone with the time to make them agree, and then say plainly what they mean.

      That is what Basirah is for. Not a report that gets filed. An answer you can act on the same day you read it.

      بصيرة Basirah is Arabic for insight: the kind of seeing that means understanding, not just looking. Most tools help you look. Basirah is built to help you understand.

      Why I started Basirah Analytics

      I’m Haseeb, and I started Basirah Analytics for a simple reason: I kept meeting owners who were sitting on the answer and could not read it. Good businesses, real numbers, and no clear way to see what those numbers were telling them. So I built Basirah to close that gap, and to be straight with people while doing it.

      A bit more about me

      I come at analysis from a finance background, so I start from the business question, not the tool. What makes money. What quietly costs more than it should. What is about to become a problem while everyone is looking the other way. Then I work back to the data to answer it.

      I try to be honest about what data can and cannot tell you. If the numbers will not support the question, I would rather say so on the first call than bill you for a report that hedges. That honesty is the whole point of the name.
