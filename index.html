      about

      Why Basirah?

      بصيرة Basirah is Arabic for insight: the kind of seeing that means understanding, not just looking.

      That is the whole idea behind the name. Most businesses are not short of data; they are short of a clear picture. The numbers are scattered across a till system, a spreadsheet, an accounting package and someone’s inbox, and nobody has the time to sit down and make them agree with each other.

      I started Basirah Analytics to turn that scattered data into a clear picture owners can actually act on. Not a report that gets filed, but an answer you can make a decision with on the same day you read it.

      Why I started Basirah Analytics

      I’m Haseeb Ur Rahman, founder of Basirah Analytics. I kept seeing business owners sitting on data they couldn’t read, so I started Basirah to turn it into answers they can act on. Basirah means insight, and that’s what Basirah is for.

      A bit more about me

      I work with small and mid-size businesses that have outgrown their spreadsheets. My background is in finance, which shapes how I approach analysis. I start from the business question: what makes money, what costs more than it should, what is about to become a problem. Then I work back to the data, rather than the other way around.

      I try to be straightforward about what data can and cannot tell you. If the numbers will not support the question, I would rather say so on the first call than bill you for a report that hedges.
