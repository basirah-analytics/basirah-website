services

From raw data to
decisions that stick.

Five stages, from a messy export to a report that runs itself. Every engagement starts with the question you need answered, not the tool.

Most projects use two or three of these, not all five. We pick the stages your problem actually needs on the first call.

01

Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before anything, the data has to be trustworthy.

Duplicated, half-formatted, contradictory data quietly breaks every report built on top of it. I get your sources into one clean, consistent base you can build on.

  • Duplicates, gaps and format errors fixed
  • Scattered sources merged into one table
  • A clear rule for how each field is defined
  • A clean dataset you can reuse, not a one-off
1 to 2 weeks
02

Data Analysis

You have the data. You need the answer.

The numbers exist somewhere. I make them agree with each other, then tell you plainly what they say and what to do about it.

  • A single reliable view of scattered data
  • The trends and outliers that actually matter
  • Customer, product and segment breakdowns
  • Written findings you can act on and share
2 to 4 weeks
03

Business & Financial Analysis

The question is about money.

Where the numbers meet the decision: what earns, what quietly costs, and what happens if you change course.

  • Profit and margin, split by what drives them
  • Pricing and unit economics you can defend
  • Forecasts and scenarios for the calls ahead
  • The handful of numbers worth watching
2 to 5 weeks
04

Dashboards & Reporting

One live view the whole team can read.

A dashboard your team reads without you, refreshed on schedule, with one agreed definition behind every number.

  • Built around your real questions, not filler charts
  • Refreshes on schedule, no manual updates
  • One agreed definition per metric
  • Built to keep running after handover
3 to 5 weeks
05

Automation & Pipelines

You rebuild the same report every week.

The repetitive copy-paste work gets replaced by a pipeline that produces the finished report before anyone asks for it.

  • Manual, repeatable reporting removed
  • Scheduled jobs that run themselves
  • One pipeline from source to finished output
  • Hours handed back to the team each week
2 to 4 weeks

      about

      Why Basirah?

      Most businesses are not short of data. They are short of a clear picture.

      The numbers are already there, scattered across a till system, a spreadsheet, an accounting package, someone’s inbox. What’s missing is someone with the time to make them agree, and then say plainly what they mean.

      That is what Basirah is for. Not a report that gets filed. An answer you can act on the same day you read it.

      بصيرة Basirah is Arabic for insight: the kind of seeing that means understanding, not just looking. Most tools help you look. Basirah is built to help you understand.

      Why I started Basirah Analytics

      I’m Haseeb, and I started Basirah Analytics for a simple reason: I kept meeting owners who were sitting on the answer and could not read it. Good businesses, real numbers, and no clear way to see what those numbers were telling them. So I built Basirah to close that gap, and to be straight with people while doing it.

      A bit more about me

      I come at analysis from a finance background, so I start from the business question, not the tool. What makes money. What quietly costs more than it should. What is about to become a problem while everyone is looking the other way. Then I work back to the data to answer it.

      I try to be honest about what data can and cannot tell you. If the numbers will not support the question, I would rather say so on the first call than bill you for a report that hedges. That honesty is the whole point of the name.
