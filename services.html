01 Understand your business

Sales & Revenue Analysis

Find out what really sells, what actually makes money, and why sales are rising or falling.

What we look at

  • Which products, services or locations bring in the most revenue
  • What sells in volume but earns very little
  • Sales by channel, month and season
  • Revenue you are losing, such as cancelled or refunded orders
  • What is rising, what is falling, and why
See a demonstration: Restaurant sales and operations analysis →

Business & Financial Analysis

See where your money goes, how healthy your margins are, and where profit is quietly leaking.

What we look at

  • Revenue, costs and profit, and how they have changed over time
  • Margins by product, customer and channel
  • Sales made below cost, and what discounts really cost you
  • Cash tied up in customers who pay late
  • Which fix to make first
See a demonstration: Rose City Roasters profitability analysis →

Customer Insights & Retention

Learn who your best customers are, how often they come back, and who has stopped buying.

What we look at

  • Your repeat rate, and how it is changing
  • Who your best customers are and what they buy
  • Customers who have gone quiet or stopped buying
  • Which channels bring customers who come back
