What happens to your data

Short version: your file is read to build your analysis, then it is gone. The detail is below, including the one thing that does leave our servers.

Your file is never stored

When you upload a file to Basira AI, it is read straight into memory, analysed, and discarded when the request finishes. It is never written to disk, never placed in a database, and never backed up. There is nothing to delete afterwards because nothing was kept. The only file our server ever reads from disk is the built-in sample dataset.

What is sent to Google Gemini

This is the part worth reading. To write the plain-English insight bullets, we send a compact statistical summary of your data to the Google Gemini API. Your file itself is never sent, and neither are your rows.

The summary does include your column names, row and column counts, aggregate figures such as totals, averages, minimums and maximums, and the three most frequent values in each text column. If a column holds customer names or product names, those top values are real values from your file. If that matters for your data, this is the thing to know before you upload.

Google processes that summary under its own API terms. If no Gemini key is configured, or the call fails, Basira AI falls back to insights computed on our own server and nothing leaves it.

What we log

Our server logs record the names of the files you upload, along with how many were received and how long the analysis took. Filenames can themselves be revealing, so rename a file before uploading if its name is sensitive. The contents of your file are never logged.

We also hold your IP address in memory for a short period to enforce usage limits and stop abuse. It is not written to disk and is discarded automatically.

What we do not do

We do not use your data to train any model. We do not sell it. We do not share your files with anyone. We do not set cookies or store anything in your browser.
